# RegTech4AI

**Bringing AI Law into Practice**

An NGF-funded project applying technical methods to the law with the aim of making the EU's ambitious AI regulation – particularly the GDPR and AI Act – work in practice.

## About

The RegTech4AI project focuses on bridging the gap between AI regulations and their practical implementation. While the GDPR is now more than 7 years old, significant gaps remain between law and practice. As the EU introduces the AI Act, RegTech4AI researches regulatory technologies (RegTech) to assist enforcement agencies and businesses with AI regulation.

## Project Aims

1. **Regulation** – Assess and improve the EU's process for AI regulation
2. **Communication** – Make knowledge around AI regulation more accessible to SMEs and start-ups
3. **Competition** – Ensure fair competition in AI and mitigate platform power
4. **Enforcement** – Build new methods and metrics to help enforcement of AI regulation at scale

## Team

The project is led by Dr. Konrad Kollnig and is part of the [Law & Tech Lab](https://www.maastrichtuniversity.nl/about-um/faculties/law/research/law-and-tech-lab) at Maastricht University.
